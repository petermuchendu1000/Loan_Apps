FAQs

Good to know.

Contact Tala support
Who can apply?

Kenyan citizens aged 18 or older who live in Kenya can apply with a valid national ID and mobile number. Tala reviews each application to determine eligibility.

Where do I apply?

Online loan applications are coming. You can explore the borrowing and repayment steps using a sample account.

How do I repay?

Open the sample account to view an example balance and try a partial or full repayment.

Can my credit limit grow?

Repaying on time can help you qualify for a higher limit over time. Your account displays your available limit after repayment.
